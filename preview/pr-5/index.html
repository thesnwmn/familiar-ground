Familiar Ground

Prototypes for a game that starts anywhere on a real world map. Start with the picker.

  • 00-picker - Pick anywhere on a real map, choose a seed, and jump into a prototype
  • 01-play - Play the real world in your choice of style: ASCII, pixel art, isometric, sketch, voxel or low-poly 3D
  • 02-gallery - Style lab: dozens of rendering styles and mashups, rendered as stills of the same real places at several zooms

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
